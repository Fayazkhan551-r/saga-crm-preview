SHOWCASE 02 · ATTENDANCE & TEAM OPERATIONS

Structured shift check-ins and attendance logs.

The Attendance module combines individual shift-start controls with a filterable multi-day attendance table so teams can access check-in controls and review attendance records in one view.

  • ✓ Shift Start & Optional Photo: Users can capture a camera photo or upload an image from their device before clicking Start Shift.
  • ✓ GPS Location Messaging: Informs users that current GPS location is captured when starting a shift and updated while online.
  • ✓ Search & Date Filtering: Search users and filter records by date range or quick presets (Today, Yesterday, Last 7 days, Current Month).
